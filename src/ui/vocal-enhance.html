Enhance Vocals

Pro-grade vocals from your sung take

How it works

  • Right-click the track holding your sung take — FXI uses its first audio clip. Consolidate the clip (⌘/Ctrl-J) if nothing is found.
  • Vocal-Max keeps your own voice: removes noise, restores detail, and polishes to a studio finish. Best on a dry recording.
  • Isolate Vocals separates any track and gives you the clean vocal — instruments and remnant bleed removed (the instrumental is the inverse).
  • Re-sing replaces your vocal with an AI performance of the same melody & lyrics in a style you choose.
  • For the cleanest result, sing a dry vocal (no heavy reverb or autotune), roughly 8–60 seconds.
  • The enhanced take drops back into your set when it's ready (about a minute).
